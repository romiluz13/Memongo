Memongo is open source, but the public product story is intentionally narrower than the full repository history.

## Public product

These are the supported product surfaces:

- `apps/api`
- `apps/mcp`
- `apps/web`
- `apps/docs`
- `packages/memory-engine`
- `packages/memory-bridge`
- `packages/memongo-memory`
- `packages/client`
- `packages/tools`

## Internal support surfaces

- `packages/lib`
- `docker/mongodb`
- `scripts/proof-pack.ts`
- `scripts/check-publishability.ts`

## Historical or planning material

Historical and planning documents are still kept in the repo, but they are not part of the main product story:

- `docs/migration`
- `docs/research`
- `docs/experiments`
- `docs/plans`
