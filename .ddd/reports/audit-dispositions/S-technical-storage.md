## Notes on PARTIAL / ambiguous cases

- **S11 (PARTIAL).** Two of the audit's three sub-claims persist verbatim: the fence is still a local boolean flipped by heartbeat failure, and no projection write carries an ownership/epoch predicate. The clock-skew sub-claim is softened: lease stamps and renewals are now server-time (`$$NOW`), and the remaining client-clock filter comparisons are explicitly documented as an NTP-assumption residual (`mongodb-memory-jobs.ts:148-152`). The core defect — a stale worker mutating projections after lease loss — is unchanged.
- **S12 (PARTIAL).** The "slow prefetch consumes the lease before ownership is checked" sub-claim is fixed: ownership is re-verified by `renewMemoryJobLease` immediately after prefetch and unowned jobs are skipped. The unbounded-reclaim sub-claim is not: the `status:"running"` claim branches carry no `attempts` bound, and although `attempts` is now incremented per claim (`mongodb-memory-jobs.ts:183`), nothing reads it on the abandoned-running path, so a crash loop can still claim forever and keep paying provider cost. The attempts-less early-fail path is also unchanged.
- **S13 (PARTIAL).** The automatic-job identity collision (the live-verified part, and the part the audit's own overlay marked repaired) is fixed at HEAD: the window-only ID became `consolidation-auto-<agentId>-<window>`. The explicit-consolidation tracking-row defect is not: `lifecycle.consolidate` still writes `status:"running"` with no `leaseToken`/`leaseExpiresAt`, and `claimMemoryJob` still treats lease-less running rows as abandoned. Request options are now at least recorded in `metadata`, but `runClaimedConsolidationJob` deliberately runs recovered jobs with defaults, so the "recovered execution lacks original options" sub-claim stands.

No STATIC_UNVERIFIABLE classifications: every finding was decidable from current source. S04/S06 live-server transition behaviors remain untested at HEAD, but the missing code paths (no collMod reconciliation, no listIndexes verification) are statically conclusive.
