## Why reassignment could be useful

The existing release supplies a tested base, transport and lifecycle repairs, and preserved failure evidence. A bounded next assignment can close specific safety or evaluation gaps using that investment. Reassignment should specify one outcome, owner decisions, cost ceiling, exact verification and stop condition. The roadmap is not a promise of competitive gains or a recommendation for unlimited funding. No deadline or completion percentage is justified by the current evidence.

## Reopening evidence gates

G1: before-failing regressions, combined tests, cross-process erase and CAS, proof positive controls, manifest drift rejection, truthful QA contracts, installed packages and independent review. The release passed a subset of these requirements, not all. G2: representative heldout tracks with licensed adapters and matched controls. G3: a chosen candidate meeting predeclared practical quality, cost and latency thresholds without correctness regressions. G4: locked confirmation, reproducible release and bounded claims. Detailed original gates remain in the private original-plan/execution/GATES.md. All gates require a newly authorized work envelope.
